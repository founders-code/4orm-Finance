## B. Publishable, and now the source of record

### The shape of the company

- **4ormIQ** is the free consumer product. **Transaction Integrity** is the
  commercial platform behind it. Mortgage brokering is the first paid market.
- The company sells to the regulated business. **The consumer is never charged,
  in any scenario.** No consumer data is sold.
- A Calgary software company. It never holds or moves client money, never signs
  a filing, and never decides anything for a customer.

### The four boundaries, verbatim from slide 24

1. Never holds or moves client money. No trust account, no payment rail, no custody.
2. Never decides or advises. The recommendation belongs to the professional.
3. Never monitors communications. Messages are contributed, and extracted facts
   stay linked to their source.
4. Never replaces the workflow. 4orm reads from the systems already running and
   writes the record beside them.

Firms hold the licence and the budget. Professionals create the records.

### What 4ormIQ answers, and what it returns

The person asks: should I send this money, who am I actually dealing with,
what does this document mean, does this offer make sense.

It returns: what was found and where it came from, what could not be verified
said plainly, the trade-offs and the missing information, and what to check
next before deciding.

**Missing information is not proof of wrongdoing, and the product never says it
is.** This is already the governing line on the phone.

### The regulatory clock, publishable

Ontario real estate brokerages file annually from **1 October 2026**, with
monthly trust reconciliation planned for 2027.
