Where the three meet

Three parties. One record. That overlap is the product.

Each of them wants a different thing from the same transaction. Trust is not a value any one of them can produce alone, and it is not a promise anybody has to take on faith. It is what is left when all three are reading the same evidence.

The whole idea

One decision. Three views.

Same transaction. Same evidence. Different view. The consumer is not looking at a different product from the broker, and the regulator is not reading a report the firm wrote afterwards. They are looking at the same record, from where they stand.
