What suitability means

Suitability is the documented path from the client’s needs to the recommendation.

It means the recommendation fits the consumer, not just the product. It asks one question: did this decision make sense for this consumer? A file has to be able to show all eight of these, in order.

01

Know the consumer

Who they are, what they are trying to achieve, and what they said matters.

02

Gather current facts

Their circumstances as they stand now, not when the file was opened.

03

Match needs to options

The product is measured against the consumer, not the other way round.

04

Compare alternatives

What else was available, and what each option would have changed.

05

Address conflicts

Who is paid by whom, and where an interest could pull the recommendation.

06

Explain the recommendation

In the consumer’s own words, before anything is agreed.

07

Confirm understanding

What the consumer actually took in, recorded as understood and accepted.

08

Preserve the record

What was known, explained, permissioned, changed and agreed, kept as it stood.

Sources: CSA Client Focused Reforms; Ontario Regulation 188/08, s. 24; FSRA.
