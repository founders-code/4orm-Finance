Who sees what

Three views of the same relationship.

The same relationship looks different depending on where you stand in it. That is deliberate.

YouEverything in your own record, including what you have shared and with whom.
The professional you connectOnly the items you authorised, and only from the moment you authorised them.
The firm they work forThe relationship events its own supervision requires, rather than the contents of your private preparation.
Nothing moves on its own. Where permission is required, you are shown what is being requested, by whom, and why, before anything is sent.
