How it works

One line, from before the relationship to long after it.

4orm is not a suite of tools. It is a single line through a financial relationship, and everything it does happens somewhere on that line.

The line

One relationship, end to end.

Most software owns one node and calls it a platform. The gap is not inside any single node. It is in the joins between them.

What the person gets

Understand it, prepare for it, check them, keep it.

01

In your own words, before anybody has anything to sell you. Ask the awkward questions here, where they cost nothing and nobody is watching.

02

Identity verified once. Income supported by a document rather than stated. The things a professional will ask for, gathered before they ask.

03

04
