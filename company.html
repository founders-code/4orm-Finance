Company

A record is only worth the people behind it.

Why 4orm is being built, the commitments that constrain what goes into it, and the three things we have deliberately decided not to build.

Why

Because financial decisions change people’s lives.

4orm is being built because the largest decisions most people make are the ones they understand least at the moment they make them, and because the firms serving them frequently did the work and cannot show it.

A person should understand what they are agreeing to.

A professional doing the right thing should be able to demonstrate it.

A firm should know how its client relationships are actually being handled.

None of those three is controversial. All three are currently hard, and they are hard for the same reason: the relationship and the record of it live in different places.
How we work

Four commitments that constrain what we build.

Sources

Every figure traces to a primary source

Nothing on this site is quoted from a summary of a summary. If a number cannot be traced to the body that published it, it does not appear.

Trends

A number that is improving is shown as improving

Nearly half of Canadian homebuyers report uncertainty. That is down from close to two thirds. Both halves of that sentence get said.

Names

We do not label anybody

This site points at published registers and published findings. It does not characterise any real person or firm, and it never will.

Limits

We say what we cannot do

4orm does not certify quality, guarantee outcomes, or control what a third party does with information downstream. Where a regulated firm has its own retention duties, those survive anything we offer.

What we are not building

Three things we have deliberately left alone.

01

Not the verification infrastructure

Identity checking, open banking, credit and signature are specialist industries with good operators in them. 4orm connects to that work rather than rebuilding it. Those are integration layers, not the reason to exist.

02

Not a replacement for the professional

The regulated judgment stays with the person licensed to make it. 4orm makes their reasoning explicable and their work demonstrable. It does not make the decision.

03

Not a surveillance product

A firm should feel it can finally see whether client relationships are being handled properly. A professional should feel it helps them take care of a client. If it ever reads as monitoring installed by an employer, we have built it wrong.
