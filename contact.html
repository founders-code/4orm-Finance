The first call

Five questions, 30 minutes.

  1. What your firm does with money that belongs to other people.
  2. Which rule that puts you under.
  3. What that rule asks for, and on what clock.
  4. How the work gets done at your firm today.
  5. Whether we can help. Sometimes we cannot.

A first call needs no customer data, and we take no copy of any. A deeper session runs under a written agreement.
