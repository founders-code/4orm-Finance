What the first call covers

In this order.

  1. What your firm does with money that belongs to other people.
  2. Which rule that puts you under, and which it does not.
  3. What the rule asks for, in plain words, and on what clock.
  4. How the work is being done at your firm today, in your own words.
  5. Whether we are any use to you. Sometimes the answer is no, and we will say so.

We do not ask for customer data to have this conversation, and we will not take a copy of any. If a deeper session is useful afterwards, it runs under a written agreement.

Who writes to us

Three kinds of message, and all three are welcome.

Most common

Does this apply to me?

You hold money for other people and you are not certain which rule you are under. We will tell you what we know and point you at the wording.

Second most common

We already know it applies

You are registered, the duty is live, and the month end is painful. This is the conversation we are best at.

Also welcome

You advise firms like these

Accountants, reviewers and counsel who sign off on this work for clients. You see the problem from the other side and we want to hear it.
