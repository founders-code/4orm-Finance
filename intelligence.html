    Why this is the hard part

    The race will not be won by whoever has the most agents.

    Capability is arriving everywhere at once and it will keep getting cheaper. What stays scarce is everything an agent has to be given before it is allowed to speak for a person.

    This is why the record comes first. The Transaction Integrity Graph is the memory and the reasoning ground, not an audit feature bolted on after the decision was made.

    Evidence that is assembled afterwards can only describe what somebody remembers. A record written as the decision happens can be reasoned over while the decision is still open, which is the only moment it can change anything.
