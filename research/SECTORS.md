## AUTO
Regulators: OMVIC (ON, Motor Vehicle Dealers Act 2002), VSA (BC, Motor Dealer Act),
AMVIC (AB, Consumer Protection Act). Lender may be federal (FCAC/OSFI) — a complaint about the
loan and a complaint about the car go to different places. That split is the story.
Registers: omvic.ca/dealer-search · vsabc.ca/salespeople/search-a-dealership-or-salesperson
Ontario all-in price rule: advertised price must include all fees the dealer intends to collect
(OMVIC Advertising Guideline, MVDA ss.28-29, O.Reg 333/08). Only HST and licensing may sit outside.

VERIFIED:
- 2,332 charges and $1.9M in fines, Ontario 2025 (OMVIC 2025 Annual Report, 4 May 2026)
- 895 charges against 174 alleged curbsiders, 2025 (OMVIC, 20 May 2026)
- $1.5M paid from the Motor Vehicle Dealers Compensation Fund in 2025, plus $825,552 returned
  through dealer compensation or refunds (OMVIC 2025 Annual Report)
- Proposals to revoke, refuse or suspend rose 34 (2024) to 79 (2025); temporary suspension orders
  3 to 26 (OMVIC 2025 Annual Report)
- Auto delinquency 0.96%, Q1 2026, up 4bps YoY (TransUnion Canada, 23 Jun 2026)

NOT VERIFIED — do not use: current average Canadian auto loan term; current negative-equity share;
add-on attach rates; dealer rate mark-up; average new-vehicle transaction price.
FCAC's 72-month and 30%-negative-equity figures are from 2015 and must be dated explicitly if used.

## LENDING
Criminal interest rate 35% APR, in force 1 Jan 2025 (SOR/2024-114). Payday exemption: cost of
borrowing capped at 14% of the amount advanced, loan under $1,500, term 62 days or less.
BC and Ontario both $14 per $100. High-cost credit threshold: 32% APR in BC and Alberta.
Registers: consumerprotectionbc.ca/payday-licence-search ·
ontario.ca/page/search-business-licence-registration-or-appointment ·
servicealberta.gov.ab.ca/consumer/business_search

VERIFIED:
- Consumer insolvencies +11.8% YoY, June 2026; bankruptcies 2,883, proposals 9,929 (OSB, 10 Aug 2026)
- Average non-mortgage debt $22,278, Q1 2026; 1.5 million consumers with a missed payment, 1 in 21
  Canadians (Equifax Canada, 26 May 2026)
- National 90+ DPD 1.86% Q1 2026, up from 1.48% in early 2022 (TransUnion Canada, 23 Jun 2026)
- Nearly one in four Canadians expect to make only minimum credit card payments
  (Equifax Canada survey n=1,532, fielded 17-19 Jul 2026, published 6 Aug 2026)
- FCAC payday study: 43% understood a payday loan is more expensive than the alternatives;
  23% took six or more loans in three years (2016 fieldwork — DATE IT EXPLICITLY)

## BANKING
FCAC does conduct. OSFI does prudential. OBSI does redress. Confusing them is the commonest
consumer error and is worth saying plainly.
Financial Consumer Protection Framework in force 30 Jun 2022: banks must offer and sell products
appropriate for their customers, deal with complaints within 56 days, ensure pay does not interfere
with that obligation, send electronic alerts, and provide separate agreements per product.
Registers: canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/industry/regulated-entities ·
canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/industry/commissioner-decisions ·
obsi.ca/en/case-data-insights/banking-case-summaries
NOTE: there is NO public register of individual bank employees. Institutions only. Say so.

VERIFIED:
- FCAC 2018 sales practices review: "Retail banking culture is predominantly focused on selling
  products and services, increasing the risk that consumers' interests are not always given the
  appropriate priority." 4,500+ complaints, 100,000+ pages, 400+ employees interviewed.
- FCAC mystery shopping (712 visits, six largest banks, Oct-Dec 2019, published 26 May 2022):
  32% of chequing shoppers and 45% of credit card shoppers reported an inappropriate product
  recommendation. Visible minority, Indigenous and student shoppers had more concerning experiences.
- FCAC complaint-handling thematic review, 31 Jul 2025: bank employees did not fully understand the
  expanded definition of complaints, resulting in incomplete record-keeping; multiple instances of
  failing to deal with complaints within 56 days; "freezing of the clock" practices.
- FCAC 2024-25: 268,718 reports from regulated entities; 190 Notices of Breach, 3 Notices of
  Violation; over $38M reimbursed to more than 745,000 accounts.
- OBSI 2025: 5,438 banking cases opened, +113% — BUT this is a mandate change (OBSI became the
  single external complaints body for all banks in 2025). Never present it as conduct deterioration.

## INVESTING

s.13.2 KYC · s.13.2.1 KYP · s.13.3 suitability determination including concentration, costs and a
reasonable range of alternatives · s.13.3(2.1) client-directed trades.
Registers: info.securities-administrators.ca/nrsmobile/NrsSearch.aspx (NRS, back to 28 Sep 2009
only) · sedarplus.ca (Disciplined List) · ciro.ca/advisor-report-search
CAVEAT TO PUBLISH: banned individuals may appear on the Disciplined List INSTEAD of NRS.

THE CENTRAL QUOTE — Joint CSA/CIRO Staff Notice 31-368, 10 Dec 2025, 105 firms reviewed:
- "Some firms had little or no documentation or recorded only that an investment was 'suitable'
   without showing the basis for that determination."
- "In many cases, registrants could not show evidence that a reasonable range of alternatives was
   considered at the time of the investment decision."
- "Many registrants did not document the suitability determination they had performed prior to
   proceeding with the client requested investment action."
- "Records should show a meaningful reassessment; generic notes like 'no changes' are insufficient."
This is the single best external validation of the 4orm thesis. The conclusion is recorded; the
reasoning is not. Use it.

VERIFIED:
- 105 registered firms reviewed (CSA/CIRO, 10 Dec 2025)
- CIRO FY2025: 4,127 complaints (up from 3,408) but PUBLIC complaints fell 229 to 159 — the rise is
  firm self-reporting, not consumers. Say so or do not use it.
- CIRO FY2025 sanctions $10,313,382, down from $14,404,764
- 13% of fines collected from individuals, down from 22%; 100% from firms
- OBSI 2025: 672 investment cases opened; of 648 closed, 31% ended with compensation;
  average $9,207, MEDIAN $990. The median is the honest number.
