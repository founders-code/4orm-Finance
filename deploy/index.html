Suitability

Businesses need to show why a recommendation fits.

Canadian requirements already connect customer information with product appropriateness. The Financial Consumer Agency of Canada expects banks to understand the consumer, assess product fit and retain records of their implemented policies. The Canadian Securities Administrators and CIRO reviewed 105 firms in 2025 and found process gaps that required action.

4orm’s proposed role: connect client context, the review and its supporting record.

01

Know the consumer

Who they are, what they are trying to achieve, and what they said matters.

02

Gather current facts

Their circumstances as they stand now, not when the file was opened.

03

Match needs to options

The product is measured against the consumer, not the other way round.

04

Compare alternatives

What else was available, and what each option would have changed.

05

Address conflicts

Who is paid by whom, and where an interest could pull the recommendation.

06

Explain the recommendation

In the consumer’s own words, before anything is agreed.

07

Confirm understanding

What the consumer actually took in, recorded as understood and accepted.

08

Preserve the record

What was known, explained, permissioned, changed and agreed, kept as it stood.

Sources: FCAC Appropriate Products and Services guideline; CSA and CIRO Client Focused Reforms review, December 2025; Ontario Regulation 188/08, s. 24. Different sector rules apply, and 105 is the review population, not the number of firms that failed.
