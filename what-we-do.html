What we do

One Place That Tells You What You Need. And Shows You It Is Done.

That is the whole ambition. Not another system to keep up to date, and not a replacement for your bank, your accountant or your accounting software. One place that knows what your regulator asks for, tells you what is outstanding, and holds the proof that the rest of it was handled. We never touch the money.

The what

Four things the software is responsible for. That is the whole list.

01 · Every day or every month

It does the checking

The comparison the rule asks for, on the schedule the rule sets, rather than when somebody has a free afternoon.

02 · As it happens

It raises the differences early

When two records disagree, that is surfaced when it appears, given to a named person, and closed with a note of what was done about it.

03 · For as long as the rule says

It keeps the proof

The record, and the wording of the rule it was made under, stay together for the whole retention period. Six years, ten years, whatever applies.

04 · When somebody asks

It produces the pack

A request covering a period from years ago is answered from the record the firm already has, rather than from a search.

A person always signs. Nothing leaves a firm until somebody with the authority to do so has read it and approved it. The software gathers and checks and drafts. A named human being decides.

The why

Three reasons we chose this over everything else we could have built.

1

The law is already in force

We are not betting on a rule being passed or on an industry agreeing to adopt something. The duty exists today, it is being supervised today, and the firms that owe it are named on a public list.

2

The work is real and it is unpleasant

Nobody enjoys the month end. Nobody enjoys reconstructing a year of records for an inspector. Software earns its place when it removes work people already resent.

3

The proof outlives the people

A review in 2028 reads records from 2026. Staff change, systems change, memories fade. A record built properly on the day is the only thing that still answers the question later.

The boundary

What we will not do, stated plainly so it can be held against us.

Never

Five things we do not do.

  1. We do not hold or move customer money. We are not a bank and we need no banking licence.
  2. We do not create evidence, and we do not change evidence that already exists.
  3. We do not decide whether a firm is compliant, and we do not certify that it is.
  4. We do not send anything to a regulator. The firm does, after a named person approves it.
  5. We do not give legal, accounting or compliance advice. Your counsel and your auditor keep their jobs.
Also worth saying

On how it works.

This site describes what the software produces and why it exists. It does not describe how it is built. Patent applications are pending, and the design is shown only under a written agreement.

That is not evasion. It is the same reason a firm does not publish its own security arrangements. If you need the detail, ask, and we will put an agreement in place first.
