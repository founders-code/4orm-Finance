What we do

Built for the day someone asks, prove it.

4orm turns compliance activity into permanent, defensible evidence. It sits alongside your bank, your accountant and your accounting software, and answers the question when an examiner names a date. We never touch the money.

What it does

Four responsibilities, and a person signs at the end of all of them.

01 · daily or monthly

It runs the check

The comparison your rule requires, on the schedule your rule sets.

02 · as it happens

It surfaces variances early

A difference between two records goes to a named person the day it appears, and closes with a note of what was done.

03 · for the retention period

It keeps the proof

The record and the wording of the rule it was made under stay together. Six years, 10 years, whatever applies to you.

04 · on request

It produces the pack

A request covering a period years back is answered from the record you already hold.

A person always signs. Nothing leaves the firm until an authorised signatory has read it and approved it. The software gathers, checks and drafts. A named human being decides.

Why we built it

Three reasons this and nothing else.

01

The law is in force

The duty exists today, it is supervised today, and the firms that owe it are named on a public list.

02

The work is unpleasant

Nobody enjoys month-end, or reconstructing a year of records for an examiner. Software earns its place by removing work people already resent.

03

Proof outlives people

A review reads records made years earlier. Staff and systems change. A record made on the day is the one that still answers the question.

What we will not do

Five limits, so you can hold us to them.

01

We never touch the money

We do not hold or move customer money. We are not a bank and we need no banking licence.

02

We never invent evidence

And we do not alter a record once it is made.

03

We do not decide whether you are compliant

And we do not certify that you are.

04

We file nothing with a regulator

Your firm does, after your signatory approves it.

05

We give no legal, accounting or compliance advice

You will still need your counsel and your auditor.
